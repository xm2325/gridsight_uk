Protocol, source evidence and limitations

The encoder is frozen. One linear head is fitted for 400 steps. A material proposal requires matching tight/context predictions, a raw logit margin of at least 0.5, and at least 16 native pixels per side / 512 square pixels. The threshold is a fixed heuristic, not calibrated reliability. Predictions classified as other/background are rejected. All original embeddings, classifier weights, logits and rejected candidates are retained.

627 previously excluded images had undefined EXIF orientation 0 with matching raw-pixel geometry and were restored to eligibility. 164 actual orientation/dimension conflicts remain excluded. Source polygons are not merged into invented whole-string labels. The original completed detector run is unchanged.
