Required architecture upgrade

  1. Proposal union: combine the material-agnostic UK-adapted detector with MPID detections to improve recall.
  2. Assembly consolidation: merge fragments only when geometry supports one physical string; reject partial or truncated assemblies.
  3. Material head: classify both a tight dielectric crop and a context crop with a supervised glass / porcelain / composite head.
  4. Evidence gate: require crop agreement, enough native pixels, assembly completeness and target-calibrated acceptance; otherwise output unknown.
  5. Prospective UK freeze: draw source-supported boxes on new asset groups before inference. Only those can support UK metrics.
