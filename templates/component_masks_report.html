Execution, provenance and remaining gaps

Original publisher polygon vertices and overlapping instances are retained. A derived diagnostic applies explicit class-aware box NMS at the already specified IoU 0.5 to remove duplicate candidates; original outputs remain selectable. This was added during development inspection, not validated on an untouched test set. Training uses 320 existing EPRI images; development uses a different circuit. The original 100-image evaluation split is not used. All 27 UK inputs are the existing qualitative set. No pseudo labels, corrected labels, new image acquisition or material training were introduced.

The model input is an exact saved RGB raster with longest side at most 1,280 pixels, resized from verified original bytes. The inspector shows this working raster, not a new native-resolution close-up. Resizing does not add evidence.

Pole-end geometry uses a sufficiently elongated pole mask and proximity to predicted crossarms/insulators. Ambiguous association or a candidate on the image edge causes abstention. It can still choose the wrong end when the component or pole mask is wrong. There are no independently labelled tip points, no physical-tip metric and no confidence score.

Better structural extent is a prerequisite for isolating steelwork, not proof that the material is steel. Steel support members/assemblies still require compatible source evidence. Corrosion, risk scoring and asset-level aggregation are not implemented by this experiment.
