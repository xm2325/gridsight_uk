Data, execution and remaining limitations

Each publisher material polygon remains a separate target. No union-of-discs targets or generated labels enter training. Unknown is an abstention state, not a third trained class. The native-pixel gate rejects proposals below 16 pixels on either side or 512 square pixels; it cannot detect every wrong or out-of-domain material prediction.

Original imagery is not sharpened or synthetically enhanced. The crop canvas displays source pixels at their original resolution internally; the browser may scale it to fit. Source annotations are independently toggled and never substituted for predictions.
